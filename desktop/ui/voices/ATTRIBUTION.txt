Voice reference samples

The WAV files in this directory were generated with Piper TTS using the following
publicly distributed voice models from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices:

- Kathleen: en/en_US/kathleen/low. Its model card identifies the source dataset
  as CC0: https://github.com/rhasspy/dataset-voice-kathleen
- Kristin: en/en_US/kristin/medium. Its model card identifies its LibriVox
  training recordings as public domain.
- John: en/en_US/john/medium. Its model card identifies its LibriVox training
  recordings as public domain.
- Bryce: en/en_US/bryce/medium. Its model card identifies its dataset as public
  domain. See https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/ for the training source.

Piper source code: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl
Voice model cards and exact models:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US

The samples were synthesized from the same original sentence specifically for
this application. Ham Study supplies the resulting samples to Qwen3-TTS as voice
references; Qwen's output is an approximation, not the original Piper voice.
